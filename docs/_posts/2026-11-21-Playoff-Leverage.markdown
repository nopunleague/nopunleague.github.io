## Week 11 Playoff Leverage
Some of you might be familiar with the playoff leverage statistic that has grown in popularity for week-by-week playoff implications of NFL match ups. 
The basic idea is that we simulate the remainder of the season, including this weeks matchups, and sort the outcomes for each team based on the outcome of this weeks matches (an informative prior, if you like Bayesian statistics).
This gives a way to quantify which teams have the most at stake this week.
Since the top of the standings are all very close in record, we can now see who still has the most at stake in the final few weeks of the regular season.
