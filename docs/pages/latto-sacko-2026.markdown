A new Sacko punishment was adopted by the NPL Member Referendum for the 2025-2026 season.
The Sackee (Latto) was tasked with entering a bowling alley of their choice and not allowed to leave until he had achieved a cumulative score, across as many games as necessary, equal to the NPL champion's regular season points total (2044 points).
In order to make this task more feasible and fun, each 12 oz. beer consumed during the punishment counted as 50 points.
There was much speculation about the strategy Latto should adopt and whether he would use bumpers or not.

Finally, the day arrived and Latto was ready to conquer his fantasy demons.
